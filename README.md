## 💬 Interview Questions & Technical Responses

### 1. What is the importance of data visualization?

It translates complex, raw data into clear visual patterns. This allows stakeholders to quickly identify trends, outliers, relationships, and critical business problems that might otherwise remain hidden in rows of data.

### 2. When do you use a pie chart vs bar chart?

Use a pie chart when comparing parts of a whole with a small number of categories. Use a bar chart when comparing values across multiple categories, such as regions or product sub-categories, because bar lengths are easier to compare accurately than angles.

### 3. How do you make visualizations more engaging?

By adding clean interactive filters, creating a logical data story, keeping the design free of clutter, and using color purposefully to highlight important differences or insights.

### 4. What is data storytelling?

Data storytelling is the practice of combining data visualizations with a structured narrative. Instead of simply showing what the numbers are, it explains what they mean, why they matter, and what business actions could be considered.

### 5. How do you avoid misleading visualizations?

Use appropriate chart types and scales, maintain consistent time intervals, provide clear labels and context, and avoid visual effects such as 3D charts or distorted sizing. For bar charts, starting the numerical axis at zero is generally recommended for accurate comparisons.

### 6. What are best practices in dashboard design?

- Organize content using a clear visual hierarchy.
- Place important KPIs where users can find them quickly.
- Limit the dashboard to relevant charts to prevent clutter.
- Use a consistent and purposeful color palette.
- Keep formatting clean and consistent.
- Hide unnecessary technical field labels.
- Use filters and interactions only when they add value.

### 7. What tools have you used for visualization?

I have used Tableau Public to build interactive dashboards, manage worksheets, apply filters and dashboard interactions, create custom actions, and publish visual data stories online.
